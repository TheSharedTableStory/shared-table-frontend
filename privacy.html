2.1 Information you provide:

(a) identity and contact details (name, email, phone);

(b) account credentials and verification records;

(c) profile information you choose to add;

(a) device identifiers, browser type, IP address, log data;

(g) improve the Platform (analytics, testing, troubleshooting);
