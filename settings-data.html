What data is stored

Here's what we have on file for your account.

    Retention window

    Account profile data is kept while your account is active. When you request deletion, personal profile fields are scrubbed and account access is disabled.

    Transaction, moderation, and policy snapshot records may be retained for financial reconciliation, dispute handling, and governance audits.

    Email & Notifications

    Choose which emails and notifications you receive.

    Booking Confirmations

    Receive email when your booking is confirmed or cancelled.

    Booking Reminders

    Get a reminder before your upcoming experience.

    New Reviews

    Get notified when someone leaves a review on your experience.

    Community Activity

    Updates about connection requests, comments, and social activity.

    Host Digest

    Daily summary of new bookings, reviews, and hosting activity.

    Promotional

    Occasional updates about new features, events, and recommendations.

    Self-serve actions

    Delete Account

    Deleting your account is permanent. All your data, bookings, reviews, and connections will be removed. This cannot be undone.

    For details on data access, correction, deletion, and retention, see our Privacy Policy.
