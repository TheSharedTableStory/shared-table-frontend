How event verification works
  1. Publish your listing and complete your host profile.
  2. Request the Verified Host badge from your My Listings page (free, ID + identity check, 3 to 5 business days).
  3. Once you have the host badge, email admin@thesharedtablestory.com with your event title, photos and any quality notes you want considered.
  4. Admin reviews the event, sets the verification fee at approval, and the fee is folded into the platform fee shown above. Guests never pay extra; the deduction comes from your payout.

Estimated payout shown above is after platform fee. If your event becomes verified, the verified-event fee is added to the platform fee at that point and reflected in your payout.
